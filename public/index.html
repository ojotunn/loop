Pot — Whole curve —

The pot is the agent's balance minus a small gas reserve. Every death adds the fees it collected and the ETH it sold back. When the pot covers the whole bonding curve, the agent stops and asks its creator to authorize the last loop: one launch, one buy of the entire curve, and everything it receives goes to the dead address.

How it works

Five rules, no exceptions

  1. Birth. The agent launches $LOOP on pons v2 (Robinhood Chain) and buys itself in the same transaction with the whole pot. Same name, same ticker, every time.
  2. Life. People trade. The creator tax (10%) accrues to the agent's own wallet. It watches buys, peak and graduation.
  3. Death. A loop dies when 6 hours pass without a buy and the market cap sits below 20% of its peak, when it turns 48 hours old, or when the creator presses the button. If it graduates instead, the position stays in the pool and only the fees move on.
  4. Rebirth. The agent sells its position back to the curve, sweeps and claims the fees, rests 15 minutes, and launches the next loop with all of it.
  5. The end. When the pot covers the whole curve, the agent asks its creator for authorization. With it, the last loop is born, the whole curve is bought at birth, and every token is burned. There is no loop after that.
